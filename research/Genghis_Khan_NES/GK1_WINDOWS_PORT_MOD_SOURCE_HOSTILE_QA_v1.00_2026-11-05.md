**Specific original-game code clues extracted from modified sources (not replacements for stock proof):**
- Full-character stats are 6 × 16-bit words at record offsets \`+0x0E..+0x18\`, Body at \`+0x16\`; entries 29 bytes for full character. The BP mod merges them **by design**. Windows Classic must retain independent values and stat-specific formulas.
- Original AI character-training path selects a weakest ability when mean below 150; new mod changes it to a 33%-maximum BP tranche *per actual training opportunity*. The mod's bounded Mesen AI routine observations do **not** prove natural AI scheduling.
- Original \`Pass\` ends remaining orders. Mod \`Rest\` consumes them and restores 33%/66%/99% per 1/2/3 orders; cap999 default is not a user approval to change Classic. Actual 257-order boundary is a **mod implementation regression**, not an original game turn budget.
- Source-review map indicates role100→3 strategic orders, other role branches→1, independent of current ability balance. Validate in stock path before port semantics closure.
- Original event transforms include duel refusal, espionage, epidemic and battle victory operating on *different components*. The mod intentionally applies each selected transform once to BP; those mod outputs are **not original-game numerical outcomes**. Original stat-specific event data and postmortem timing must be extracted from prepatch instructions.
- The mod intentionally treats zero BP as recoverable and uses age255 for death; original all-six-zero sentinel is different. Never silently import this fix into stock game, and verify legitimate age/death conditions from unmodified code.
- Demobilization is mod-only. Original Draft and Hire/Labor are useful comparative accounting control paths. One native mod battle showed the 10 soldiers per strategic troop relationship in its tested state, not complete combat AI or every battle outcome.
- The candidate replaced ~3,139 bytes in 80 explicit regions between the two mod ROMs. The complete 80-patch intervals provide a strong map of *which original code was replaced or relocated*, but cannot prove unchanged whole-game correctness.
- The four-season approved HD pack preserves its original image/rule bytes in the new basename for a mod; it has no audio extraction and does not make 43 unresolved graphics-loader sites disappear.
