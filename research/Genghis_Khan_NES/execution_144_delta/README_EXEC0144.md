# EXEC0144 — Authenticated archived-state PRG mapping evidence, not fresh reset

This is a new linked C11 module in the complete inherited native game, not a new isolated prototype or a gameplay unlock. `gk144_archived_mapper.c/h` parses **uncompressed named-field MesenCE data** only after the existing GK139 CPU/RAM-source checks pass. It decodes 128 memory-manager 256-byte pages at $8000–$FFFF and cross-checks mapped physical PRG-ROM offsets, ROM page type/access, saved MMC1 PRG register, mode, and selected fixed slot. It strictly refuses inconsistent or unsupported mapping, duplicate keys, malformed data, and leaves output unchanged. `gk1_app_source_archived_mapper` is non-mutating and setup-gated.

Private ORIGINAL STOCK Mesen archived Mongol1175 and World1206 saved-state evidence was independently extracted, and each showed **low PRG-ROM bank 0 and fixed high bank 15 at its saved instant**, with mapper.prgReg=0, prgMode=1, slotSelect=1 and CPU PC=$EFC9. These are **OBSERVED_RUNTIME_STOCK_ARCHIVED_RESTORED**, NOT a fresh stock power-on reset or a runtime trace of Spring setup. The fresh original Spring 1175 record bank is nesWorkRam, archived records are nesSaveRam; these cannot be interchanged. No startup bank, setup RNG/NMI chronology, first Spring, or orders are inferred.

Synthetic hostile tests include every valid low-bank mapping 0..14, corrupt 256B page offsets, truncated/duplicate field refusal, source-stage gate, and unchanged app/output.
